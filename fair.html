SATONG Will Attend the 140th Canton Fair | Phase 1, Booth 18.1F31

Published: September 21, 2026 | Exhibition News

We are pleased to announce that SATONG will officially participate in the 140th Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair) in October 2026. As one of the most influential international trade exhibitions in China, the Canton Fair gathers global buyers, suppliers and industry elites from manufacturing, logistics, warehousing and automation fields.

Exhibition Information

Exhibition Name: 140th Canton Fair

Phase: Phase 1 (Advanced Manufacturing Session)

Booth No.: 18.1F31, Hall D

Exhibition Time: October 15 – October 19, 2026

Location: Pazhou Complex, Guangzhou, China

What We Will Display

At this exhibition, SATONG will showcase our core warehouse storage products and intelligent logistics solutions, including heavy-duty pallet racking, mezzanine flooring, shuttle racking systems, and customized automated warehouse solutions.

Our professional engineering and sales team will be on-site to provide one-to-one technical consultation, warehouse layout design, project solution discussion and factory cooperation negotiation for global customers.

Why Visit Our Booth

With more than 18 years of manufacturing and exporting experience, SATONG focuses on high-quality warehouse racking and intelligent storage system R&D and production. Our products have been widely exported to Europe, South America, Central Asia, the Middle East, Southeast Asia and other global markets.

We provide free warehouse design, customized storage solutions, strict quality control and reliable after-sales service for distributors, logistics companies and manufacturing enterprises worldwide.

We sincerely welcome new and old customers from all over the world to visit our booth, communicate face to face, discuss project cooperation, and explore long-term win-win partnership opportunities.
