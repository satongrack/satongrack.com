About Us

Jiangsu Satong Intelligent Logistics Equipment Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer integrating R&D, design, production, sales and installation of warehouse storage equipment. Founded in 2006, the company has 20 years of manufacturing experience, covering an area of more than 30,000 square meters. We have passed ISO9001 quality management system certification, CE certification and multiple national patents. We focus on providing high-quality standard and customized warehouse racking products and intelligent storage system solutions for global customers, covering Europe, America, Southeast Asia, Middle East, Central Asia and other regions. Adhering to the principle of quality first and service-oriented, we have won long-term trust and praise from customers worldwide.

Industry Solutions

Cold Chain & Food Industry Solution

Professional anti-rust, low-temperature resistant racking solutions, suitable for cold storage, food processing and fresh food storage scenarios, ensuring storage safety and long service life in low-temperature and humid environments.

Printing Industry Solution

Customized special racks for printing materials, suitable for ink trays, printing plates, sample films and paper storage, realizing classified storage and efficient access of printing supplies.

Furniture Industry Solution

Heavy-duty cantilever racks and mezzanine platforms for furniture, wooden materials and long-size goods, solving the storage problems of large, long and irregular furniture products.

Core Products

Pallet Racking

Standard heavy-duty pallet racks, high load capacity, flexible assembly, suitable for most warehouse pallet storage scenarios, the most widely used storage rack.

Cantilever Racking

Designed for long, tubular and irregular goods, widely used in steel, wood, aluminum profile and building materials storage.

Drive-In Racking

High-density storage racks, no aisle partition, greatly improving warehouse space utilization, suitable for large-volume single-variety goods storage.

Mezzanine Racking

Make full use of warehouse vertical space, build multi-layer storage platforms, suitable for low-rise warehouse space expansion and small goods storage.

Shuttle Racking System

Semi-automatic intelligent storage system, high efficiency and high density, reducing manual operation and improving warehouse automation level.

AS/RS Automated Warehouse

Fully automatic intelligent storage system, realizing unmanned access, inventory counting and intelligent management, suitable for large modern logistics warehouses.

Push-Back Racking

Sequential access storage structure, automatic goods sliding, suitable for multi-batch and small-batch goods storage, efficient and labor-saving.

Gravity Flow Racking

Gravity self-sliding design, realizing first-in first-out (FIFO) storage, very suitable for food, medicine and time-sensitive goods storage.

Frequently Asked Questions
1. Do you provide customized warehouse rack solutions?
Yes, we provide one-stop customized services including scheme design, structural optimization, production manufacturing and on-site installation. We customize products according to your warehouse size, floor load, goods specifications and storage requirements to meet personalized storage needs.
2. What is your product warranty period?
We provide 5-year warranty for all standard racking products and lifelong after-sales technical support. Damaged parts caused by non-human factors will be replaced free of charge within the warranty period.
3. Are you a factory or trading company?
We are a professional direct manufacturer with 20 years of production experience. Our factory covers 30,000 square meters, with complete production equipment, independent R&D team and strict quality inspection system, supporting factory direct sales.
4. What certifications do your products have?
Our products have passed ISO9001 quality management system certification, CE certification, SGS inspection and multiple national utility model and invention patents, meeting European and international export standards.
5. What is your production lead time?
Standard products can be delivered within 7-10 working days. For customized products and large-scale engineering projects, the production cycle is 15-30 working days, depending on the project quantity and customization difficulty.
6. Do you provide overseas installation service?
Yes. We can provide overseas on-site installation guidance service, and also support remote video installation guidance. We have professional international project teams with rich experience in overseas project docking.
7. What payment terms do you accept?
We support multiple payment terms: T/T, L/C, Western Union, etc. The conventional term is 30% deposit in advance and 70% balance before delivery.
8. Can you provide free design and quotation?
Absolutely free. We provide free professional warehouse scheme design, product matching and detailed quotation according to your actual needs, with no hidden charges.
9. What is the maximum load capacity of your pallet racks?
Our standard pallet racks can bear 500kg-5000kg per layer. We can customize super heavy-duty racks according to customer load requirements to meet heavy-load industrial storage needs.
10. Do your products support ocean shipping and container loading?
All products are designed for export packaging, supporting FCL and LCL ocean shipping. We optimize the packaging and loading scheme to save container space and reduce your logistics costs.
