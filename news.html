Extended answers to high-frequency questions from overseas warehouse racking buyers.

Pallet racking is ideal for multi-SKU storage with direct access to each pallet. Drive-in racking is best for large-volume, single-variety goods and maximizes space utilization. If your goods are diverse, choose pallet racking; if your goods are uniform and space is limited, choose drive-in racking.
